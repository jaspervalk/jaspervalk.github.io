SubsidieMatch

SubsidieMatch reads Dutch contractor quotes, extracts equipment with Claude Vision, matches against 7,729 RVO subsidy codes, and returns exact meldcodes and amounts in about 30 seconds.

8 meldcodes · 3 regelingen · ~30s

  1. PDF Vision Extraction

    Same PDF must always return same matches. Solved with SHA-256 content hashing + version-pinned prompts. Bit-for-bit reproducible.

  2. Subsidy Matching

    7,729 meldcodes, hundreds of brands. Predicted € 7,172 on a real quote; RVO paid € 7,856. 91.3% accuracy on first production run.

  3. WBSO Quickscan

    Classifies R&D activities against WBSO criteria with section-level citations from the RVO handleiding. 3 of 4 activities were correctly classified as eligible on the first real client document.

  4. Arbitrage Engine

    Found € 4,664 in missed subsidy on a single offerte by detecting a kozijn + triple glass combination the basic matcher couldn't see.
